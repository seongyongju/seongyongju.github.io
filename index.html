I am an undergraduate at Chung-Ang University, studying Industrial Security with a double major in Mathematics and an interdisciplinary major in Cyber Security.

I am interested in building mathematical algorithms, through the lens of inference, learning, and optimization, that cut the overhead and latency of secure AI systems. I currently work with Yunheung Paek at the Security Optimization Research Lab, Seoul National University, on confidential computing for GPUs and secure HW/SW architectures for automotive controllers.

Previously, I worked on federated learning and LLM jailbreak mechanisms with Jaewoo Lee, and on LLM agent security with Hoki Kim at Chung-Ang University.
