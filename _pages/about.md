I am an Undergraduate in the Department of Industrial Security at Chung-Ang University, double-majoring in Mathematics and pursuing an interdisciplinary major in Cyber Security.

My recent research interest lies in developing mathematical algorithms from the perspectives of inference, learning, and optimization, with the goal of optimizing AI security within the Safe AI domain. with my Advisor [Yunheung Paek](http://sor.snu.ac.kr) in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Seoul National University.

Previously, I conducted research on Safe AI, for Federated Learning and LLM Jailbreak Mechanism analysis, with [Jaewoo Lee](https://sites.google.com/) as my advisor. I also conducted research on Agent Bias with [Hoki Kim](https://trustworthyai.co.kr) as my advisor. in addition, security optimization in hardware architecture design, primarily focusing on how to minimize overhead when introducing TEE or TrustZone into controller architectures for mobility in SDV environments.​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​​
